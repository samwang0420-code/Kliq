Refund policy

Kliq Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. If it isn't right for you, ask for your money back within 30 days — no interrogation, no forms.

Last updated: 2026-09-20

The short version

  • 30 days from purchase — full refund, any reason.
  • Email your payment receipt to hi@yanjingai.tech.
  • We refund to your original payment method within 5 business days.
  • Your licence key is deactivated when the refund is issued.
  • Kliq is AGPL-3.0 source-available — you can always build it yourself for free.

What is covered

This policy covers the Kliq Pro one-time licence (US$12.90) sold through our checkout. It does not cover any charges you pay to third-party AI providers.

Requesting a refund

Email hi@yanjingai.tech with the receipt you received at purchase. A two-line message is enough:

  • The email address or order number used at checkout
  • Your licence key, if activation already succeeded

Send it within 30 days of the purchase date. Requests after that window are considered on a case-by-case basis, but we can't guarantee them.

What happens to your licence

When a refund is issued, the licence key is deactivated and can no longer activate or re-validate Kliq Pro. The app itself keeps working — it just falls back to the free feature set. Recordings, projects and exports you already made are yours permanently; we never touch them.

When you'll get the money

We approve refunds within 2 business days of your request. The payment processor then returns the funds to your original payment method, which usually lands within 5–10 business days depending on your bank or card issuer. Refunds are issued in the original currency, so a small difference may appear if your card's exchange rate moved.

Third-party costs are not refundable

Kliq's AI features call your own API account — we don't proxy or resell AI usage, and we never see your key. Any spend at OpenAI, DeepSeek or another provider is billed by them and is outside this policy. Transcription and AI actions consume your provider quota only while you use them.

Not sure AI is worth the extra cost? The free edition records and edits without any AI at all — try that first, then decide.

Your statutory rights

This 30-day policy is more generous than the minimum cooling-off periods required in most jurisdictions, including the EU/UK right of withdrawal. Nothing here limits rights you have under mandatory local consumer law. If local law gives you a longer window or stronger remedy, that law prevails.

Chargebacks

Please email us before filing a chargeback — a refund is faster for everyone. We do not fight reasonable requests, and we would rather hand the money back than run a dispute.

Free and open source

Kliq is licensed AGPL-3.0. The complete source is public at github.com/samwang0420-code/Kliq, so paying is never the only way to use it — Pro exists for people who want the signed builds and would rather support the work than maintain a fork.

Questions

Anything unclear about this policy, or a refund that's taking too long? Write to hi@yanjingai.tech and we'll answer.
